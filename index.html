NEED TO KNOW SOMETHING?

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some questions you may have.

Can I request a customised design?

Absolutely! We welcome personalised requests. You can share your preferred design, colours, inspiration images and other details when making your enquiry.

How do I know the price of an item?

Prices depend on the product and the level of customisation required. Some items may have fixed prices, while personalised creations may require a quotation.

How early should I place my order?

We recommend placing orders early, especially for customised cakes, gifts and event-related requests. The required preparation time may vary depending on the order.

Do you handle event decorations?

Yes! We offer event decoration and styling. Share your event type, date, location and ideas with us so we can discuss your requirements.

Can I request a special gift package?

Yes. You can tell us the occasion, your preferred items, colours and budget, and we'll discuss a suitable gift arrangement.
