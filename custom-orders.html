Custom Orders Policy

Every special request deserves attention to detail. Discover how we bring your personalised ideas and creative requests to life.

Home / Custom Orders
YOUR IDEAS, OUR CREATIVITY

Let's Create Something Special Together.

At HAFHASS Events & Delights, we understand that some moments call for something more personal and unique.

Whether you need a customised cake, personalised frame, special gift, bouquet, event decoration or another creative product, we aim to understand your ideas and work towards bringing them to life.

This policy explains how custom orders are discussed, confirmed, priced, prepared and delivered, as well as the responsibilities of both parties throughout the process.

01

Understanding Custom Orders

A custom order is a product or service prepared or arranged according to a customer's specific preferences, instructions or requirements.

Depending on the request, custom orders may include:

  • Customised cakes, pastries and other food products.
  • Personalised frames and gift items.
  • Custom bouquets and gift arrangements.
  • Special product combinations or packaging.
  • Personalised designs, names, colours and messages.
  • Event decoration, styling and special arrangements.
  • Other creative products or services offered by our business.

Each request is considered according to its nature, complexity, availability of materials and our ability to fulfil the agreed requirements.

02

Making a Custom Order Request

Customers may submit custom order requests through the available ordering channels or by contacting our team.

To help us understand your request, please provide as much relevant information as possible.

This may include:

  • The product or service you want.
  • Your preferred design, theme or concept.
  • Colours, sizes, quantities and measurements.
  • Names, dates, messages or other personalised details.
  • Reference photographs or design inspiration.
  • Your preferred completion or delivery date.
  • Your estimated budget, where applicable.

Providing clear information helps us assess your request and determine the most suitable way to proceed.

03

Reference Images and Design Inspiration

Customers may provide photographs, sketches, screenshots or other reference materials to communicate their preferred design.

Reference materials help us understand the desired appearance, style, colours and overall direction of a custom order.

However, a reference image is generally treated as inspiration and a guide rather than a guarantee that the final product will be an exact reproduction.

Differences may occur because of available materials, production techniques, equipment, ingredient availability and the nature of handmade work.

Where an important design detail cannot reasonably be reproduced, we will endeavour to discuss an appropriate alternative with the customer.

04

Availability and Feasibility of Requests

Not every custom request can necessarily be fulfilled exactly as submitted.

Before accepting a custom order, we may consider:

  • Availability of the required materials and ingredients.
  • Availability of suitable equipment and resources.
  • The complexity and technical requirements of the design.
  • The requested quantity and production capacity.
  • The time available before the required date.
  • Whether the requested product or service is currently offered.

If we cannot fulfil a particular request, we may suggest an alternative where reasonably possible.

A custom request is not considered accepted until the relevant details and arrangements have been confirmed by our team.

05

Custom Order Pricing and Quotations

Custom order prices may differ from the prices of standard products because additional materials, preparation, labour or specialised work may be required.

Depending on the request, the price may be based on:

  • The type of product or service.
  • Size, quantity and complexity.
  • Materials, ingredients and special components.
  • Personalisation and design requirements.
  • Additional preparation or production time.
  • Packaging and other agreed arrangements.

Where a quotation is required, we will communicate the proposed price before the customer confirms the order.

Customers should review the quotation and any included specifications carefully before making payment.

Any additional work or changes that affect the agreed price will be discussed with the customer before proceeding.

06

Order Confirmation and Acceptance

A custom order becomes confirmed when the relevant requirements and payment arrangements have been agreed upon and our team has accepted the order.

Depending on the order, confirmation may involve:

  • The agreed product or service.
  • Design and personalisation details.
  • Quantity, size and other specifications.
  • Quoted price and payment arrangements.
  • Expected preparation or completion date.
  • Delivery or collection arrangements.

Customers should not assume that submitting a request or making an enquiry automatically confirms an order.

We will communicate when the order has been accepted and what steps are required before production begins.

07

Design Review and Customer Approval

Where a custom order involves a design, layout, wording or other specifications that can be reviewed before production, customers may be asked to approve the relevant details.

Customers should carefully check all information presented for approval, including:

  • Names and spelling.
  • Dates and numerical details.
  • Colours and design choices.
  • Measurements and dimensions.
  • Wording, messages and inscriptions.
  • Other personalised instructions.

Once the customer approves the relevant details, we may proceed with production based on that approval.

Customers are encouraged to raise any concerns before giving final approval.

08

Production Time and Completion Dates

Custom orders may require more preparation time than standard products.

The expected completion time may depend on:

  • The complexity of the request.
  • The quantity and size of the order.
  • Availability of materials and ingredients.
  • Current production commitments.
  • Required design approvals or customer feedback.
  • Other relevant preparation requirements.

Customers should communicate their required dates as early as possible.

Any completion date communicated by our team should be treated as an estimate unless a specific date or time has been expressly confirmed as guaranteed.

We will make reasonable efforts to inform customers if circumstances arise that may affect the agreed preparation schedule.

09

Payment, Deposits and Balance Payments

Custom orders may require full payment, a deposit or another agreed payment arrangement before production begins.

The payment arrangement will depend on the nature, value and preparation requirements of the order.

Where a deposit is required, the amount and any remaining balance will be communicated to the customer before confirmation.

Customers should ensure that payments are made through the payment channels provided or approved by our business.

Production will commence according to the payment and confirmation arrangements communicated for the particular order.

Any cancellation or refund request involving a deposit or part-payment will be assessed in accordance with our Refund & Cancellation Policy and applicable law.

Read our Refund & Cancellation Policy
10

Changes, Revisions and Additional Requests

We understand that customers may sometimes wish to make adjustments after submitting a custom order.

Requests for changes should be communicated as early as possible.

Whether a change can be accommodated may depend on:

  • Whether production has commenced.
  • The stage of completion.
  • Availability of additional materials.
  • Whether the change affects the original design.
  • Additional time, labour or costs required.

Some changes may require an additional payment or an adjustment to the expected completion date.

We will communicate any relevant changes to the price or timeline before proceeding with additional work.

Changes cannot always be accommodated once production has reached an advanced stage or the order has been completed.

11

Handmade Products and Natural Variations

Some of our products involve handmade preparation, decoration or creative work.

As a result, minor variations may occur between a reference image, an approved design and the finished product.

These variations may relate to:

  • Colour shades and tones.
  • Decorative placement and arrangement.
  • Finishing and presentation.
  • Natural ingredient characteristics.
  • Other features associated with handmade production.

We aim to maintain the agreed design direction and quality standards while recognising that handmade work may not be identical in every detail.

Minor reasonable variations do not automatically mean that an order is defective or incorrectly supplied.

However, any material difference from the confirmed agreement or genuine quality concern will be considered fairly.

12

Customer Responsibilities

Customers play an important role in ensuring that custom orders are completed according to the agreed requirements.

Customers are expected to:

  • Provide accurate and complete order information.
  • Submit clear design instructions and references.
  • Review quotations and specifications carefully.
  • Check names, spelling, dates and other personalised details.
  • Respond to approval requests within a reasonable time.
  • Make agreed payments according to the communicated arrangements.
  • Communicate requested changes promptly.
  • Provide accurate delivery or collection information.

Delays in receiving required information, approvals or payments may affect the expected completion date.

13

Unavailable Materials and Alternative Arrangements

Occasionally, a particular ingredient, material, colour, decorative component or other requested item may become unavailable.

Where this happens, we will consider whether a suitable alternative can reasonably be offered.

If the alternative would materially affect the agreed appearance, function or value of the order, we will endeavour to discuss the proposed change with the customer before proceeding.

We will not knowingly make a material change to an agreed custom order without appropriate communication with the customer.

14

Cancellation of Custom Orders

Customers who wish to cancel a custom order should contact us as soon as possible.

Cancellation requests will be assessed according to the stage of preparation or production and the circumstances of the particular order.

We may consider:

  • Whether production has commenced.
  • The amount of work already completed.
  • Materials or ingredients already purchased or used.
  • Labour and other costs already incurred.
  • Whether the personalised product can reasonably be reused or resold.
  • Any separate agreement made with the customer.

Depending on the circumstances, a full refund, partial refund or another reasonable arrangement may be considered.

Custom orders are not automatically non-refundable in every situation. Requests will be assessed fairly and in accordance with applicable law.

View our Refund & Cancellation Policy
15

Delivery and Collection of Custom Orders

Once a custom order is completed, the delivery or collection arrangements agreed upon with the customer will apply.

Customers should ensure that someone is available to receive or collect the order at the agreed time.

Certain products, especially cakes, food items, delicate decorations and personalised gifts, may require careful handling during transportation.

Customers should follow any relevant handling, storage or collection instructions provided by our team.

Additional delivery arrangements or charges may apply where the original delivery details need to be changed.

Read our Delivery & Collection Policy
16

Quality Concerns and Order Issues

We take care to prepare custom orders according to the agreed specifications.

If a customer receives an order that is damaged, defective, incorrect or materially different from what was confirmed, please contact us promptly.

To help us review the concern, customers may be asked to provide:

  • The relevant order reference.
  • A description of the issue.
  • Photographs or videos showing the concern.
  • Other relevant information about the order.

We will review the circumstances and consider an appropriate resolution, which may include correction, replacement or refund where suitable.

Nothing in this policy removes any rights or remedies available to customers under applicable law.

Learn about refunds and replacements
17

Photographs and Display of Custom Work

We may photograph completed custom products for internal records, quality documentation or portfolio purposes.

Photographs of products or completed work that do not identify the customer or another individual may be used for promotional or portfolio purposes unless the customer has requested that the work remain private.

We will seek separate permission before using photographs that show identifiable customers, recipients or other individuals in promotional materials.

Customers who prefer their custom orders to remain private should communicate this request when placing the order or before the work is shared publicly.

We will take reasonable account of such requests.

18

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Custom Orders Policy to reflect changes in our products, services, production practices or applicable legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page.

Where a change materially affects an existing order or separate agreement, we will take reasonable steps to communicate the relevant change to the affected customer.
