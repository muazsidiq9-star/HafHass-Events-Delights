YOUR INFORMATION, OUR RESPONSIBILITY

We Respect Your Privacy

At HAFHASS Events & Delights, we value your trust and take the privacy of our customers seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal information when you visit our website, create an account, place an order, request a service or communicate with us.

By using our website or services, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

Last updated: November 2026

01

Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us provide our products and services, manage customer accounts, process orders and communicate with customers.

Personal Information

This may include:

  • Your full name.
  • Email address.
  • Telephone number and WhatsApp contact details.
  • Delivery address and location.
  • Account information and preferences.
  • Information you provide when contacting us.

Order and Transaction Information

When you place an order or request a service, we may collect information such as:

  • Products and services requested.
  • Order quantities and selected options.
  • Customisation details and special instructions.
  • Delivery or collection preferences.
  • Order history and transaction records.
  • Payment status and relevant payment references.

Uploaded Images and Reference Materials

You may upload photographs, designs, documents or other reference materials to help us understand your requirements for customised products and services.

These materials are used to understand, prepare and fulfil your requests. Please avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive or confidential information.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Processing and managing orders.
  • Confirming payments and updating payment records.
  • Preparing customised products and services.
  • Arranging deliveries and customer collections.
  • Communicating order confirmations and updates.
  • Responding to enquiries, complaints and requests.
  • Improving our website, products and customer experience.
  • Preventing fraudulent activities and unauthorised access.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We will not knowingly use your personal information for purposes unrelated to our business without an appropriate legal basis or your consent where required.

03

Customer Accounts

Customers may create accounts on our website to access account-related features and manage their orders.

When creating an account, you are responsible for providing accurate information and keeping your login credentials confidential.

You should notify us if you suspect that someone has accessed your account without your permission.

We may use your account information to identify you, provide customer support and maintain your order history.

04

Payments and Financial Information

We collect and maintain relevant payment information needed to confirm and manage transactions.

Depending on the payment method used, transactions may be handled through banks, payment providers or other relevant financial service providers.

We may retain payment references, transaction details, payment confirmations and records necessary for accounting, customer support and legal obligations.

We do not request your banking password, card PIN or other confidential authentication credentials. You should never share such information with anyone claiming to represent us.

05

When We Share Your Information

We respect the confidentiality of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

However, we may share limited information when reasonably necessary to operate our business and fulfil your requests.

This may involve:

  • Delivery personnel and logistics providers who need relevant details to deliver your order.
  • Payment providers and financial institutions involved in processing or confirming transactions.
  • Technology and service providers who help us operate our website, manage customer accounts or provide essential business services.
  • Relevant authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful request.

We aim to limit shared information to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

06

Promotional Messages and Marketing

We may use your contact information to send announcements, promotional messages, special offers, product updates and other business-related communications.

You may opt out of promotional communications at any time by using an available unsubscribe option or contacting us directly.

Opting out of promotional messages will not prevent us from sending important communications relating to your account, payments, orders or services.

07

Photographs, Customer Work and Marketing

We may photograph or document products, completed orders, decorations and other work created by HAFHASS for business records, portfolios and promotional purposes.

Where a photograph shows only a product, completed order or our work without identifying an individual, we may use it for marketing and promotional purposes unless the customer has requested that the particular work remain private.

Photographs or videos containing identifiable people are not covered by this general permission. We will seek separate affirmative permission before using such photographs or videos for marketing.

Customers may also request that specific orders, customised work or event projects not be displayed publicly or used for promotional purposes.

We respect agreed privacy restrictions and will take reasonable steps to honour them.

08

How We Store Your Information

Customer information may be stored electronically through our website systems, databases and relevant service providers.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Maintain customer accounts.
  • Manage current and previous orders.
  • Resolve complaints and disputes.
  • Maintain financial and business records.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Where information is no longer reasonably required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or otherwise dispose of it securely, subject to applicable legal requirements.

09

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or misuse.

Access to customer information is limited to individuals and service providers who reasonably need it to perform their responsibilities.

Although we work to protect your information, no website, electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should also take reasonable steps to protect your account credentials and personal devices.

10

Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and improve your browsing experience.

These technologies may help the website maintain necessary settings, remember preferences and support features such as customer accounts and shopping functionality.

You may be able to manage or restrict cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

Where non-essential cookies or tracking technologies requiring consent are introduced, we will provide appropriate information and choices.

11

Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally appropriate.
  • Object to certain uses of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request information about how your personal information is used.
  • Raise concerns about the handling of your personal information.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions and other applicable requirements.

To exercise a privacy right or make a request, please contact us using the details provided below.

12

Account Deletion and Data Retention

You may request the deletion of your customer account by contacting us.

Once we receive your request, we will take reasonable steps to process it in accordance with applicable law.

Please understand that deleting an account does not necessarily mean that every record associated with it can be immediately erased.

We may need to retain certain information for legitimate business purposes, unresolved transactions, dispute resolution, financial records or legal and regulatory obligations.

Where continued retention is necessary, we will handle the retained information in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

13

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for individuals who can legally enter into transactions or use the relevant services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental or guardian consent is legally required but has not been obtained.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

14

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms or third-party services.

These external platforms operate under their own privacy policies and practices.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites that we do not control.

We encourage you to review the relevant privacy policies before providing personal information to external services.

15

International Data Processing

Some technology or service providers that support our website may process or store information using systems located outside Nigeria.

Where personal information is transferred or processed outside Nigeria, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing complies with applicable data protection requirements.

16

Legal and Regulatory Compliance

We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy laws in Nigeria, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any privacy rights that you may have under applicable law.

17

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, website features, technology or applicable legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date.

Where a significant change affects how we handle existing customer information, we will take reasonable steps to communicate the change where appropriate.

18
