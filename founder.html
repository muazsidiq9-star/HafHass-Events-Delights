MEET THE WOMAN BEHIND THE BRAND

A Creative Heart With A Beautiful Dream.

I am Kadiri Hafsah Hassanah

Most people know me as Hafsah, while some call me Hassanah. I am a baker, a cook, and a creative at heart.

I have always dreamed of creating beautiful things for people through food, thoughtful gifts, and event decorations.

That dream existed long before I had the skills or opportunity to bring it to life. Today, through HAFHASS Events & Delights, I get to turn that dream into beautiful experiences for others.

With love, KADIRI HAFSAH HASSANAH Founder, HAFHASS Events & Delights

THE BEGINNING

Every Great Journey Starts Somewhere.

Before the beautiful creations, before the growing customer base, there was simply a dream and the desire to make people happy.

A Dream That Started Small

The Dream Came First.

Whenever someone close to me had a birthday, graduation, or another special occasion, I always wished I could create something beautiful for them.

I wanted to bake their cake, arrange something special, and make them feel celebrated. I had the desire, but I didn't yet have the skills or the opportunity to learn.

Alhamdulillah, that changed when I met a professional who gave me the opportunity to learn.

Under her guidance, I developed my skills in baking, cooking, presentation, and decoration. That experience became a turning point in my journey.

I had the desire, but I did not yet have the skills. Then I finally found the opportunity to learn.

THE JOURNEY SO FAR

From Humble Beginnings To Beautiful Possibilities.

HAFHASS did not start big. It grew through determination, learning, consistency, and the support of people who believed in the dream.

THE FIRST STEPS

Starting Small

HAFHASS started from home with chin chin, egg rolls, and chips. In those early days, there were just one or two customers.

Every order was prepared, packaged with love, and delivered with care.

LEARNING AND GROWTH

Growing Through Challenges

The journey was not always easy. Financial challenges sometimes made it difficult to purchase ingredients and materials needed for training and practical sessions.

But the desire to learn remained stronger. With determination, she continued learning, practising, and trusting the process.

A MEMORABLE MILESTONE

The First Stranger Who Trusted Her

One unforgettable moment was receiving a cake order from someone who was neither family nor a friend.

That trust made her realise that HAFHASS was becoming more than a personal hobby.

ANOTHER MILESTONE

The First Full Event Decoration

Seeing the hall she had styled, beautifully transformed, while people took pictures, was another unforgettable experience.

In that moment, she paused and said, Alhamdulillah.

TODAY

A Growing World of Creativity

From those first small orders, HAFHASS has expanded into cakes, pies, parfaits, yoghurt, chin chin, shawarma, barbecues, bouquets, event decorations, and more.

What began as a simple dream continues to grow into something beautiful.

WHAT INSPIRES US

The Heart Behind Every Creation.

For Hafsah, it has never been only about making products. It is about creating moments that make people feel special.

What I enjoy most is seeing someone's face light up.

I know what it feels like to want to make someone happy but not know how. Now, I have the opportunity to create those special moments for others.

Love inspires my creativity. Whenever someone tells me that an order is for their mother, best friend, spouse, or someone special, I remember how I used to wish I could create something beautiful for my own loved ones.

That feeling inspires me to put genuine care into every creation.

Every Occasion Matters.

Whether it is a simple treat, a thoughtful bouquet, or an entire event setup, I want every customer to feel celebrated.

LOOKING AHEAD

A Bigger Dream. A Brighter Future.

My biggest dream is to build HAFHASS into a brand people trust for all their celebrations.

I envision HAFHASS with its own shop and creative studio, a well-trained team, and the capacity to cater for beautiful weddings, corporate events, and other major occasions.

Beyond building a successful business, I want HAFHASS to inspire other young women who have dreams but aren't sure where to begin.

I want them to know that starting small does not make a dream small. With the right guidance, consistent practice, determination, and trust in Allah, something beautiful can grow.

THE DREAM

To Become a Trusted Name Behind Beautiful Celebrations.

From everyday delights to unforgettable occasions, we dream of making every celebration more beautiful.
