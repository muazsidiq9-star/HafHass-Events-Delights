FAIRNESS AND TRANSPARENCY

Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want you to feel confident when ordering from us. Learn how we handle cancellations, refunds and replacements.

Home / Refund & Cancellation Policy
OUR COMMITMENT TO YOU

Your Satisfaction Matters to Us.

At HAFHASS Events & Delights, we take care to prepare and deliver products and services according to the details agreed upon with our customers.

We understand that circumstances can change and that issues may occasionally arise. This policy explains how customers can request cancellations, refunds or replacements.

Every request will be considered fairly, taking into account the circumstances of the order, the work already completed and applicable consumer protection laws.

01

General Refund and Cancellation Principles

We aim to handle refund and cancellation requests reasonably and transparently.

Whether a refund, replacement or cancellation can be approved may depend on:

  • The type of product or service ordered.
  • Whether preparation, production or service delivery has commenced.
  • The stage of completion at the time of the request.
  • Materials, labour and other costs already incurred.
  • The reason for the request.
  • Any separate agreement made with the customer.
  • Applicable consumer protection laws.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove consumer rights provided by applicable law.

02

Cancellation Before Preparation or Production

Customers may request cancellation as long as preparation, production or the relevant service has not commenced.

We do not impose one universal cancellation deadline for every order. The important consideration is whether work on the order has already started.

If cancellation is requested before work begins, we will review the request and communicate any applicable refund arrangements.

Customers should contact us as soon as possible when they wish to cancel an order.

03

Cancellation After Work Has Commenced

Once preparation, production or service delivery has started, cancellation requests will be assessed based on the circumstances of the order.

We may consider:

  • The amount of work already completed.
  • Materials purchased or used specifically for the order.
  • Labour and other costs already incurred.
  • Whether the product or service can reasonably be reused or resold.
  • The terms of any separate agreement with the customer.

Depending on these circumstances, a full refund, partial refund or other reasonable arrangement may be considered.

We will explain the basis of our decision to the customer rather than automatically treating every cancellation as non-refundable.

04

Customised Products and Special Orders

Customised products are prepared according to details supplied and approved by the customer.

Customers are encouraged to carefully review all design details, colours, sizes, quantities, names, dates, wording and other specifications before production begins.

If a customer requests cancellation of a customised order after production has started, we will assess the request based on the work completed, materials used and other costs incurred.

A personalised product may not always be suitable for resale or reuse. This may affect the available cancellation or refund arrangement.

However, each case will be considered on its own circumstances and in accordance with applicable law.

05

Event Bookings and Services

Event styling, decoration, catering arrangements and other advance bookings may involve planning, preparation, reservations, purchases and commitments made before the event date.

Such bookings may have separate cancellation arrangements agreed upon before confirmation.

These arrangements may cover:

  • Event date and location.
  • Agreed services and scope of work.
  • Deposit and balance payment arrangements.
  • Preparation and booking commitments.
  • Cancellation and rescheduling conditions.

Customers should review and confirm any separate booking agreement before making payment.

06

When a Refund or Replacement May Be Considered

We may consider a refund or replacement where there is a genuine problem attributable to HAFHASS.

Examples include:

  • An item arrives damaged due to our handling or preparation.
  • A product has a genuine defect attributable to us.
  • The wrong product or order is supplied.
  • The delivered product materially differs from the confirmed order due to our error.
  • An agreed service is not provided as promised due to our fault.

We will review the circumstances and determine whether a refund, replacement, correction or another appropriate solution is suitable.

This does not prevent customers from exercising any other rights available under applicable law.

07

Reporting Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Orders

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect order, please contact us within 24 hours of receiving the order.

To help us investigate and resolve the issue, please provide:

  • Your name and order reference.
  • A clear description of the problem.
  • Photographs or videos showing the issue.
  • Any other relevant information requested by our team.

Please retain the affected item and its packaging where reasonably possible until we have reviewed the complaint and advised you on the next steps.

We will assess the evidence and communicate an appropriate resolution.

The 24-hour reporting period is intended to help us investigate issues promptly. It does not override any mandatory rights or remedies available under applicable law.

08

How Approved Refunds Are Processed

Where a refund is approved, we will normally endeavour to return the money through the original payment method where possible.

If the original payment method is no longer accessible or cannot reasonably receive the refund, we may ask the customer to provide another accessible payment method.

Customers may be asked to provide information reasonably necessary to verify the transaction and process the refund securely.

The time required for the refund to reflect may depend on the relevant bank, payment provider or financial institution.

We will communicate relevant refund arrangements to the customer.

09

Replacement and Correction of Orders

Depending on the nature of a confirmed issue, we may offer to:

  • Replace the affected product.
  • Correct an incorrect order.
  • Arrange another suitable solution.
  • Provide a refund where appropriate.

The chosen resolution will depend on the nature of the issue, the product or service involved, practical arrangements and applicable law.

Where a replacement or correction is agreed, we will communicate the expected arrangements and timeline.

10

Situations Requiring Further Assessment

Some situations may require additional review before a refund or replacement can be considered.

Examples include:

  • A customer changes their mind after customised production has commenced.
  • Incorrect names, spelling, dates, measurements or other details were supplied or approved by the customer.
  • A customer requests changes after the relevant production stage has passed.
  • An order cannot be delivered because incorrect delivery details were provided or the customer was unavailable to receive it.
  • A concern relates to a reasonable variation in the appearance of a handmade product.

These circumstances will not automatically determine the outcome of a complaint. We will consider the specific facts, any agreements made and applicable consumer rights.

11

Perishable Products and Food Items

Cakes, pastries, parfaits, yoghurt and other food products may require particular storage, handling and consumption conditions.

Customers should follow any storage, collection or handling instructions provided with their orders.

If a food item is damaged, defective or incorrect when received, please notify us promptly and provide clear evidence of the issue.

We may need to consider the condition of the product, the time of notification and any relevant handling or storage circumstances when reviewing the complaint.

Nothing in this section limits any applicable consumer rights relating to product quality or safety.

12

How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To request a cancellation, refund or replacement, please contact us through our available customer support channels.

Include the following details where applicable:

  • Your full name.
  • Order reference or transaction details.
  • The product or service concerned.
  • Whether you are requesting cancellation, refund or replacement.
  • A clear explanation of your request.
  • Supporting photographs or videos where relevant.

Providing complete information will help us review your request more efficiently.

13

Review and Resolution of Requests

Every request will be reviewed based on the information available and the circumstances of the order.

We may contact you for clarification or additional evidence where necessary.

Once a decision has been reached, we will communicate the outcome and any agreed next steps.

We aim to handle complaints professionally and resolve genuine concerns fairly.

14

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy to reflect changes in our business practices, services or applicable legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date.

Where a change materially affects an existing order or separate agreement, we will take reasonable steps to communicate the relevant change to the affected customer.
