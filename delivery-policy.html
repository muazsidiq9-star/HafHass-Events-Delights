Delivery & Collection Policy

We want your orders to reach you safely and in good condition. Here's what you need to know about our delivery and collection arrangements.

Home / Delivery & Collection Policy
A SMOOTH DELIVERY EXPERIENCE

Every Order Deserves Careful Handling.

At HAFHASS Events & Delights, we understand that receiving your order is an important part of your shopping experience.

Whether you choose delivery or collection, we aim to prepare your order carefully and communicate the relevant arrangements clearly.

This policy explains how our delivery and collection arrangements work, including delivery areas, fees, estimated timelines and customer responsibilities.

01

General Delivery and Collection Information

We offer delivery and, where available, collection arrangements for eligible orders.

The available fulfilment method may depend on:

  • The customer's delivery location.
  • The type and quantity of products ordered.
  • The preparation requirements of the order.
  • Product availability and readiness.
  • Delivery arrangements available at the time.
  • Any special arrangements agreed upon with the customer.

Available delivery and collection options will be communicated during the ordering process or confirmed by our team where necessary.

02

Delivery Areas and Availability

Delivery availability depends on the location provided by the customer and the delivery areas we can currently serve.

Not every location may be eligible for delivery. Some areas may require additional arrangements or confirmation before an order can be accepted.

Before completing an order, customers should provide accurate delivery information so that availability can be checked.

If delivery is unavailable for a particular location, we may discuss alternative arrangements where reasonably possible.

03

Delivery Fees and Charges

Delivery fees may vary depending on the destination, distance, delivery arrangements and other relevant factors.

Where delivery fees are available through our website, the applicable fee will be displayed during the ordering or checkout process.

Where a delivery fee cannot be determined automatically, our team may need to confirm the charge with the customer before the order is finalised.

Customers are responsible for reviewing the delivery fee and the total order amount before completing payment.

We will communicate any additional delivery charges before proceeding with an arrangement that requires the customer's approval.

04

Delivery Times and Estimated Arrival

Delivery times may vary depending on the order, preparation requirements, destination, availability of delivery personnel and other circumstances.

Any delivery date or arrival time communicated to a customer should be treated as an estimate unless a specific time has been expressly confirmed as guaranteed.

Some orders may require additional preparation time, particularly customised products, large orders and event-related arrangements.

We encourage customers to place orders early where a product or service is needed for a particular date or occasion.

If we become aware of a significant delay, we will make reasonable efforts to inform the affected customer.

05

Order Preparation Before Dispatch

Orders are prepared according to the products, quantities, specifications and other details confirmed by the customer.

An order may require preparation, packaging, quality checks or other arrangements before it is ready for dispatch or collection.

For customised products and special orders, preparation may take longer depending on the nature and complexity of the work.

We will communicate any important preparation requirements or expected readiness arrangements where necessary.

Customers should not assume that an order is ready for collection or dispatch until they have received confirmation from us.

06

Providing Accurate Delivery Information

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate delivery details when placing an order.

Where applicable, this includes:

  • The recipient's full name.
  • A valid contact telephone number.
  • The complete delivery address.
  • The relevant area, street or landmark.
  • Any useful directions needed to locate the destination.
  • Special delivery instructions where necessary.

Customers should review their delivery details carefully before submitting an order.

If any information changes after an order has been placed, please contact us immediately.

Changes may not always be possible once an order has been dispatched or a delivery arrangement has been confirmed.

07

Receiving Your Delivery

Customers should ensure that someone is available to receive the order at the agreed delivery location and within the communicated delivery period.

The recipient may be asked to provide reasonable confirmation of their identity or order details before the order is handed over.

Where another person is authorised to receive an order on the customer's behalf, the customer should ensure that the person is available and properly informed.

Customers should provide any relevant access instructions in advance, particularly for locations with restricted entry or security procedures.

08

Failed or Unsuccessful Delivery Attempts

A delivery may be unsuccessful where:

  • The recipient is unavailable at the delivery location.
  • The contact number provided cannot be reached.
  • The delivery address is incomplete or incorrect.
  • The delivery location cannot reasonably be accessed.
  • The recipient refuses to accept the order.
  • Other circumstances prevent the delivery from being completed.

Where a delivery attempt is unsuccessful, we will make reasonable efforts to contact the customer and discuss the available next steps.

A further delivery attempt may require a new delivery arrangement and may attract an additional fee where applicable.

Any additional charges will be communicated to the customer before a new delivery arrangement is confirmed.

09

Order Collection

Where collection is available, customers may arrange to collect their orders from the designated collection location.

Collection details, including the location and expected readiness time, will be communicated by our team.

Customers should wait for confirmation that their order is ready before travelling to collect it.

When collecting an order, customers may be asked to provide their name, order reference or other reasonable confirmation.

If someone else will collect the order on the customer's behalf, please inform us in advance.

10

Delayed Collection and Uncollected Orders

Customers are encouraged to collect their orders within the collection period communicated by our team.

If a customer is unable to collect an order at the agreed time, they should contact us as early as possible to discuss alternative arrangements.

Delayed collection may affect the condition or suitability of certain products, especially food items and other perishable goods.

Where an order remains uncollected, we may contact the customer to agree on reasonable next steps.

Any additional storage, handling or redelivery arrangements will be discussed with the customer where applicable.

11

Food Items and Perishable Products

Cakes, pastries, parfaits, yoghurt and other food products may require careful handling, storage and timely collection or delivery.

We take reasonable care when preparing and packaging food orders for delivery or collection.

Customers should:

  • Receive food orders promptly.
  • Follow any storage instructions provided.
  • Keep products under the recommended conditions.
  • Avoid unnecessary exposure to heat or unsuitable environments.
  • Follow any relevant consumption guidance.

Once an order has been received, customers are responsible for following the handling and storage instructions provided.

If a food item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, please contact us promptly so that we can review the matter.

12

Checking Your Order Upon Receipt

We encourage customers to check their orders as soon as reasonably possible after delivery or collection.

Please verify that:

  • The items received correspond with the order.
  • The quantities supplied are correct.
  • The products appear to be in acceptable condition.
  • Any agreed customisation details are reasonably accurate.

If you notice damage, missing items, incorrect products or another issue, please notify us promptly.

Photographs, videos and your order reference may help us investigate and resolve the issue.

Our Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how complaints, replacements and refund requests are handled.

Read our Refund & Cancellation Policy
13

Unexpected Delays and Delivery Disruptions

Although we make reasonable efforts to fulfil delivery arrangements as planned, circumstances beyond our reasonable control may sometimes cause delays or disruptions.

These may include:

  • Unexpected traffic or road conditions.
  • Severe weather conditions.
  • Vehicle breakdowns or transport disruptions.
  • Security restrictions or access difficulties.
  • Unexpected operational or logistical challenges.
  • Other circumstances affecting safe or timely delivery.

Where such circumstances affect an order, we will make reasonable efforts to inform the customer and discuss alternative arrangements where possible.

We appreciate customers' understanding when delays occur for reasons beyond our reasonable control.

14

Large Orders, Custom Orders and Event Deliveries

Large orders, customised products and event-related deliveries may require additional planning and coordination.

These orders may involve:

  • Special preparation and packaging arrangements.
  • Specific delivery dates and time windows.
  • Additional transportation requirements.
  • Special handling for delicate or perishable items.
  • Coordination with event organisers or designated recipients.

Customers should communicate important dates, deadlines, venue details and other requirements before confirming such orders.

Any special delivery or collection arrangements should be agreed upon with our team in advance.

Where a separate agreement applies, its relevant delivery arrangements will also be considered.

15

Changes to Delivery or Collection Arrangements

Customers who need to change their delivery address, contact details, collection time or other fulfilment information should contact us as soon as possible.

We will consider reasonable requests where the order has not progressed beyond the stage at which the change can be accommodated.

Changes may not always be possible after dispatch, preparation or other relevant arrangements have commenced.

A change in delivery location may also affect the delivery fee or availability of the service.

We will communicate any relevant adjustments before confirming the revised arrangement.

16

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Delivery & Collection Policy to reflect changes in our delivery arrangements, business operations or applicable legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page.

Where a change materially affects an existing order or separate agreement, we will take reasonable steps to communicate the relevant change to the affected customer.

17

Contact Us

If you have questions about delivery, collection, delivery fees or an existing order, please contact us.
